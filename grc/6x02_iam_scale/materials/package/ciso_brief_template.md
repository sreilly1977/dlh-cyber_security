# CISO Brief Template

## Identity Posture Summary
State audit results in organizational risk terms and include Critical/High counts.

## Connection to the Incident
Name findings that recreate the prior lateral movement path. Cite finding IDs.

## What Has Been Completed
Examples: corrected IAM policies, SAML SSO analysis, Vault dynamic credential design, repeatable IAM audit script.

## What Must Be Completed
Three actions with owner roles and dates.

## What Requires Board Authorization or Budget
Name funding/authorization and risk if deferred.

## Board Resolution Requested
One sentence authorizing a specific action.
