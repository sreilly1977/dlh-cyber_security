# Clinical Schedule Constraints

**Owner:** Sarah Park  
**Purpose:** Prevent simulation campaigns from interfering with patient care or accreditation workload.

## General Constraints

- Do not send new simulation waves during Joint Commission accreditation window.
- Do not send new clinical simulations during flu-season peak between November and January.
- Existing microtraining may remain available during restricted windows.
- Do not target units during known staffing shortages.
- Do not run simulations during declared downtime or active incident response.

## Shift Windows

| Group | Safer Delivery Window | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| General floor nurses | 07:15-08:30 or 19:15-20:30 for baseline lures | medication pass peak, active code events |
| ICU nurses | Coordinate with unit leadership; avoid broad sends | shift handoff during surge |
| ED staff | Use limited sample only | trauma surge, high census alerts |
| Laboratory | 09:30-11:00 for non-STAT themes | STAT backlog periods |
| Radiology | 10:00-11:30 | emergency imaging surge |
| Billing/admin | Tuesday-Thursday 09:00-11:00 | payroll close, month-end |
| Finance | Tuesday-Thursday 10:00-14:00 | month-end close, audit deadlines |
| Executives | Coordinate with executive assistant | board meeting days |

## Cool-Down Rules

- Same population should not receive another simulation within 21 days.
- Employees who clicked should receive microtraining immediately, not another lure.
- Repeat-click evaluation requires consecutive waves, not multiple clicks in the same wave.

## Exclusions

Temporarily exclude:

- employees on leave
- employees in first week of employment
- staff involved in active patient safety event
- staff assigned to incident response
- employees under HR-approved support plan where simulation would be inappropriate

## Program Design Implication

Campaign design must balance realism with operational safety. The point is to train behavior, not to create unsafe clinical distraction.
