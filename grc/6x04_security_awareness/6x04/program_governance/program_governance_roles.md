# Program Governance Roles

## Role Table

| Role | Responsibilities |
|---|---|
| CISO | Approves program strategy, board reporting and high-risk templates |
| IT Security Awareness Lead | Runs campaigns, metrics, microtraining and reporting |
| SOC Manager | Reviews reported simulations and operational false positives |
| HR Director | Owns privacy boundary, coaching process and non-punitive policy |
| Legal Counsel | Reviews insider threat and vishing escalation procedures |
| Clinical Operations Representative | Reviews clinical timing and patient-care safety constraints |
| Finance Representative | Reviews BEC and payment-verification scenarios |
| Communications Lead | Ensures plain-language employee messaging |
| Department Managers | Receive aggregate metrics and support training availability |

## Approval Flow

1. Threat profile drafted by IT Security.
2. HR reviews privacy and employee impact.
3. Clinical or business owner reviews operational realism.
4. Legal reviews high-risk simulations and insider threat content.
5. CISO approves launch.
6. Board receives quarterly metrics.

## Governance Rules

- No template launches without sign-off.
- No individual results in performance reviews.
- Managers receive aggregate data.
- HR escalation requires documented repeat pattern.
- Legal review required before anonymous insider reporting launch.
- Campaign metrics must be reviewed quarterly.
