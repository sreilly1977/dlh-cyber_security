## Risk Reduction Rationale

The Q1 incident required IR activation, endpoint containment, forensic analysis, credential reset and executive communications. Reducing click rate and increasing report speed decreases attacker dwell time and downstream containment cost.

## Cost-Justification Metrics

| Metric | Baseline | Year 1 Target | Board Relevance |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Click rate | 56% | 25% or lower | Human-layer exposure |
| Report rate | 15% | 50% or higher | Early warning capability |
| Mean time to report | 74 min | 15 min or less | Detection speed |
| Repeat clicker rate | Unknown | <8% | Coaching effectiveness |
| Training completion after click | Not measured | 95% within 72h | Intervention completion |

## Funding Decision

If the board does not fund a platform, MedDefense can still run manual campaigns, but metrics quality, segmentation, landing-page response and HR privacy controls will be weaker.
