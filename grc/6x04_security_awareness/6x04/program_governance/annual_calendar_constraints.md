# Annual Calendar Constraints

## Scheduling Constraints

- 90-day initial rollout begins after CISO and HR sign-off.
- No new clinical training content during Joint Commission accreditation window.
- No new clinical simulations during flu season peak: November through January.
- Existing content and reinforcement reminders may continue during restricted windows.
- Finance simulations must avoid month-end close.
- Executive simulations must avoid board meeting days.
- IT simulations must avoid active incident response periods.

## Suggested First-Year Calendar

| Month | Recommended Activity | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Month 1 | Threat profile, template review, baseline phishing wave | Include SEG-B and SEG-C |
| Month 2 | Post-click intervention and reporting reinforcement | Focus on report behavior |
| Month 3 | Wave 2 and vishing tabletop | Include clinical supervisors |
| Month 4 | Role-based quick guides rollout | All staff |
| Month 5 | Finance/executive BEC simulation | SEG-E and SEG-F |
| Month 6 | Insider threat awareness manager training | Managers, IT, HR |
| Month 7 | Wave 3 intermediate campaign | Avoid clinical overload |
| Month 8 | Remote worker security campaign | Remote staff |
| Month 9 | Program metrics review | CISO and HR |
| Month 10 | Restricted clinical window | Reinforcement only |
| Month 11 | Restricted flu-season window | No new clinical campaigns |
| Month 12 | Annual board metrics and next-year plan | Budget decision |

## Success Criteria

- Reduced click rate
- Increased report rate
- Reduced mean time to report
- Reduced repeat clicker rate
- Improved manager confidence in escalation
- Board receives quarterly metrics
