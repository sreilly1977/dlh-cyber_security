# Manager Reference Constraints

## Manager Responsibilities

Managers are responsible for noticing operational context that tools may not know:

- role changes
- contractor end dates
- unusual access requests
- behavior inconsistent with assigned duties
- repeated attempts to bypass process
- signs of confusion after security simulation

## What Managers Should Document

- date and time
- observable behavior
- system or process involved
- witnesses if any
- business context
- whether immediate safety risk exists

## What Managers Should Not Do

- do not confront the employee as a suspected insider
- do not search personal devices
- do not tell the team about a concern
- do not include simulation clicks in performance review
- do not make conclusions about motive
- do not delay urgent security reporting to "handle it locally"

## When To Escalate

Escalate to IT Security or HR when:

- the issue involves access to systems or patient records
- the behavior repeats
- a vendor or employee requests unusual privilege
- a contractor or transferred employee still has access
- the employee reports distress or fear after a simulation

## Tone Requirement

Manager guidance must preserve trust. The program is not surveillance. It is a safety and security reporting framework.
