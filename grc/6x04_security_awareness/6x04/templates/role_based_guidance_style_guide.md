# Role-Based Guidance Style Guide

## Universal Structure

Each one-page guidance document must include:

- Security Quick Reference: [Audience]
- MedDefense branding placeholder
- version and date
- Your biggest threats
- What to do right now
- When something looks wrong
- What not to do
- footer with ext. 4-SECURITY, security@meddefense.org and Outlook report button

## Clinical Staff Voice

Use short, direct, workflow language.

Good:
- "Do not enter Epic credentials from an email link."
- "If a vendor calls during an equipment issue, call back using the approved number."

Avoid:
- "Validate digital identity assertions."
- "Apply zero-trust principles."

## IT Staff Voice

Assume technical competence. Use operator language.

Good:
- "Do not run admin actions from personal email, personal devices or untracked sessions."
- "Challenge service accounts with no owner, no ticket and no recent expected activity."

Avoid:
- generic reminders like "be careful online"

## Executive Voice

Use risk and decision language.

Good:
- "Any request involving money, credentials or confidential documents requires independent verification."
- "Your account can authorize operational damage beyond your inbox."

Avoid:
- technical jargon and long checklists

## Remote Worker Voice

Use home and travel context.

Good:
- "Connect VPN before opening work systems."
- "Do not move work files into personal cloud storage to make them easier to access."

## Reading Time

Each guide should be readable in 90 seconds.
