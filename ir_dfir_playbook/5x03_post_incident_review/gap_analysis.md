## Priority summary

- **GAP-001** (P1): The phishing domain was publicly blacklisted six days before the click; blocklist-enforced DNS and web filtering would have stopped the intrusion at its first step with zero tuning effort.
- **GAP-002** (P1): The conditional access policy was actively cancelling correct risk output; restructuring rule precedence so risky sign-ins require MFA regardless of device compliance is the single change that stops this entire chain replaying.
- **GAP-004** (P1): Enforcing WDAC against LOLBin execution from Temp directories would have blocked the MSBuildLoader stage outright, independent of any detection coverage.
- **GAP-007** (P1): Ten hours of C2 to infrastructure flagged across three public intelligence sources went unblocked; forcing workstation egress through a feed-consuming gateway converts that visibility gap into a hard stop.
- **GAP-009** (P1): Credential Guard and RunAsPPL were not enforced; enabling them breaks the exact credential-access step (NTLM hash exposure in lsass) that enabled lateral movement to WST-WS-017 and the PHI access.
- **GAP-011** (P1): A standard workstation account silently read PHI on the clinical file server; segmenting LIS infrastructure and tightening the LIS_REPORTS$ ACL directly protects the regulated data category the attackers targeted.
